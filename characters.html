Characters

The cast of Gidi++: Baba Gidi, the people he carries across, and the officers who make it harder. On the left, the reference image the 3D model was built from. On the right, our treatment: the shipped model rendered in the Lagos dusk grade. Semi-real, 12+, no real people.

How to read this page. REFERENCE = an existing project image (pipeline concept input or a render of our own model), hosted because it's project-made. IN-GAME MODEL = our treatment: the shipped model re-staged at Lagos dusk (warm key light, cool rim, asphalt, soft shadows) in a three.js stage. Same mesh and texture as the build. CONCEPT = a new design proposal, not in the game yet. No real people are depicted: every human is one of our own generated in-game models. Provenance is listed in each reference/*/README.md.

Hero, archetypes and officers

Baba Gidi

Hero. Green kaftan + fila, the default fit. Reads from the 65° camera: gold-trimmed fila and white placket lines on green.

Slay Queen (Corporate)

Ped type SlayQueen. Leather jacket + orange bob. Moves fast and dodges; the bob gives her a warm hair colour that reads on asphalt.

Slay Queen (Casual)

Variant 1: graphic tee, ripped jeans, natural hair.

Agbada Man (Bigman)

Ped type AgbadaMan. Navy agbada with gold chain. Slow and heavy, so he's worth more when you deliver him.

Agbada Man (Chief)

Variant 1: burgundy agbada over a navy kaftan.

Agbada Man (Ankara shirt)

Variant 2: Ankara shirt + trousers (casual bigman).

LASTMA officer

Faction AI. Hi-vis lime vest, navy trousers, peaked cap. Flags and fines you; Change settles am. VO: “Oga, park well!”

KAI officer

Faction AI. Khaki uniform + tactical vest, beret. Clears hawkers and crowds in zones.

Commuters (the crowd)

Eleven generic commuters fill the crowd you lead. Each card shows reference (top) and in-game model at dusk (bottom).

Hawkers CONCEPT

Needed for Gist Bomb (“Have you heard?!”, vision v2 §8): hawkers' gossip pulls LASTMA/KAI officers off their posts. These concepts reuse shipped commuter bodies with head trays, so no new rig is needed. Tray props are about 1.5k tris (props budget, v2 §4).
