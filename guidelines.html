Guidelines

Rules every screen follows. The source is G-Prototype docs/ui/ArtDirection_OnePager.md and UX_Flows_Sprint1.md.

Layout

  • Landscape only. Reference canvas 1920×1080, CanvasScaler matched on height.
  • Everything sits inside Screen.safeArea. Desktop margin 32 px. Phone (19.5:9, 2340×1080): notch inset 132 px, home indicator 63 px.
  • Minimum touch target 144 px = 48 dp. Primary CTA 192 px tall.
  • Thumb zones: bottom-left (translucent PICK UP) and bottom-right (translucent 8-way d-pad, primary CTA). Pause and Settings top-right, where they need a deliberate reach.
  • In-run: camera and controls as in the current build (overlap of controls with lane ends accepted). See the HUD spec.

HUD

  • Stock order is always GROUP → CHANGE → OWA!, left to right.
  • Currency is always ₦ + amount (₦12,450), never "N".
  • Continue: max 1 per run. Cost: 100 chips at L1, 125 chips at L2+, or watch an ad (GidiEconomyTables).
  • Touch controls only on touch devices. No keyboard / "Space" hints on screen.

Accessibility

  • Warning states pair colour with shape, icon and motion (see Palette).
  • Text contrast is WCAG AA or better on glass; graphics are at least 3:1.
  • Planned: colour-blind mode, text size S/M/L, reduce motion, haptics toggle.

Don't

  • No portrait layouts. No kid-style bubbly UI. No real brands.
  • No economy numbers that aren't in GidiEconomyTables.
  • Never rely on colour alone. Don't move controls away from the build positions without sign-off.
