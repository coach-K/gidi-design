Gidi++ brand

Cross Lagos. Don't get jammed. A 3D (Unity URP, semi-real, perspective camera) curb-crossing game for Millennials and Gen Z. You play Baba Gidi (12+, not a kids' game). You lead a crowd across Lagos traffic, from Agege to the endless chaos of Ojuelegba.

Pillars

  • Semi-real Lagos dusk-glass. Grounded proportions and believable materials, pushed about 20% toward readability. Ink glass UI, warm gold, hairline edges.
  • Readable threats first. Every danger is shown by colour and shape and motion (colour-blind safe).
  • Fair free-to-play. Soft-currency power-ups (₦ Naira Chips), 1 continue per run, cosmetics only for real money.
  • Proudly Lagos. Real places and Pidgin/Yoruba flavour ("Eiya!", "Owa!", "Enter with your change!"), with fictional brands only.

Landscape only on every device (Kehinde, Oct 16, 2026, RT-106). Cars cross sideways, so landscape shows whole lanes. There are no portrait layouts.
