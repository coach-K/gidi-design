Costumes & skins

Baba Gidi outfits, rendered on the shipped Baba Gidi model and rig at Lagos dusk. Fits are texture swaps plus, for agbada, a robe layer. The Humanoid rig, silhouette and hitbox don't change. These replace the earlier flat drawings.

How to read this page. REFERENCE = an existing project image (pipeline concept input or a render of our own model), hosted because it's project-made. IN-GAME MODEL = our treatment: the shipped model re-staged at Lagos dusk (warm key light, cool rim, asphalt, soft shadows) in a three.js stage. Same mesh and texture as the build. CONCEPT = a new design proposal, not in the game yet. No real people are depicted: every human is one of our own generated in-game models. Provenance is listed in each reference/*/README.md.
Sources. Vision v1: “Baba Gidi outfits (agbada, okada helmet, Ankara fits, football jerseys in generic styles, no licensed kits)” and “Detty December … owambe outfits”. IAP SKUs in GidiEconomyTables.IapSkus: pack_baba_fits and pack_skins_lagos. Cosmetics are vanity only. Prices and pack contents: TBD (not in the vision doc).
