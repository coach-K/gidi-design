## Canvas
- CanvasScaler: Scale With Screen Size, reference **1920×1080**, match **height (1.0)**, landscape only. On a 2340×1080 phone, 1 ref px = 1 device px; at 3x, **3 px = 1 dp**.
- Safe area: desktop 32 px margin. Phone: `Screen.safeArea` (mock: left/right 132 px = 44 dp notch inset, bottom 63 px = 21 dp home indicator, top 24 px). All HUD lives inside the safe rect.
- Minimum touch target **144 px (48 dp)**. Font: Plus Jakarta Sans Bold.

## Camera framing
- Pitch **65°** (`TailCameraFollow.fixedEulerAngles`), yaw 0, X locked. Scrolls on Z with the crowd.
- **Vertical FOV 50–55°, fixed.** Unity FOV is vertical, so a wider aspect shows more lane length: about 38 m visible at 19.5:9 versus about 32 m at 16:9.
- Fit `lanes × 4 m` (lane Z spacing in TrafficManager) plus 1.5 m curb top and bottom into the road band, which is about 46–49% of screen height. Use a viewport-aware offset, i.e. aim the camera so the road band's centre lands at screen y ≈ 0.42.
- Lane margin: keep at least 1.5 car lengths of lane visible on both sides of the crowd. At 11–16 m/s lane speeds, cars appear about 1.3–1.7 s before they reach the crowd.
- 8-lane levels: raise camera height (15 m → about 20 m) or widen FOV up to 60°. Never shrink the HUD.
- World telegraphs (Danger chevrons, Caution triangle) stay inside the safe area horizontally.
