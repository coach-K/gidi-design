## Canvas
- CanvasScaler: Scale With Screen Size, reference **1920×1080**, match **height (1.0)**, landscape only. On a 2340×1080 phone, 1 ref px = 1 device px; at 3x, **3 px = 1 dp**.
- Safe area: desktop 32 px margin. Phone: `Screen.safeArea` (mock: left/right 132 px = 44 dp notch inset, bottom 63 px = 21 dp home indicator, top 24 px). All HUD lives inside the safe rect.
- Minimum touch target **144 px (48 dp)**. Font: Plus Jakarta Sans Bold.

**Known compromise:** with the crowd at about 78–85% of the height, the 300 px PICK UP and the d-pad overlap the outer screen-edge ends of the lane just ahead of the crowd. Cars there have already been visible for most of their run. Options: (a) accept it, (b) aim the camera so the crowd sits at about 90%, or (c) shrink PICK UP to 216 px (still above 144). Recommendation: (b).

## Camera framing (real 3D perspective camera, URP)
- **Perspective** camera behind and above Baba Gidi (not orthographic, not 2D). Current settings: `TailCameraFollow` offset (0, 15, −5), pitch **65°**, yaw 0, X locked, follows the crowd on Z. The L8 capture shows this: lanes run left–right and converge slightly toward the top.
- **Vertical FOV fixed at 50–55°.** Unity FOV is vertical, so a wider screen shows more lane length: 19.5:9 shows the most, 16:9 a little less.
- **Aim:** the far curb / goal sits **below y = 200** (under the HUD row is shoulder or beyond-goal, as in the mock, where the top 200 px is a darkened shoulder fill representing this proposal). The crowd sits at about 80–90% of the height, so the screen is mostly lanes still to cross.
- **Width:** the farthest active lane should span at least 70% of the screen width so cars are visible early. On 7–8 lane levels (L7–L10), raise the camera height (15 → about 20 m) or move it back before widening the FOV. Never shrink the HUD.
- World telegraphs (headlight flash, chevrons, LASTMA diamond) stay inside the safe area horizontally.
