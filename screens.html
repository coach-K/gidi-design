Gidi++ is a 3D game (Unity URP, semi-real, perspective camera over the road and Baba Gidi). The scenes in these mockups are drawn (tools/render_scene.py: a pinhole render at the build camera: pitch 65°, 15 m / FOV 52°, or 20 m / FOV 58° on 8-lane stages), using the build captures in reference/levels/ only as reference for camera, scale and controls. The controls match the build: a translucent gold-outlined PICK UP bottom-left and a translucent 8-direction d-pad bottom-right.
